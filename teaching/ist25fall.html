Intelligent Speech Technology

This course introduces core theory and modern practice in speech technology, from signal processing and ASR to speaker modeling, speech synthesis, audio and music processing, responsible AI, and speech agents.

Teaching Format
Lectures + demos + project-oriented practice

As one of the most natural forms of human communication, speech is central to the next generation of AI systems. This course provides a structured path from fundamentals to state-of-the-art, covering both classical and neural approaches. We discuss practical design choices used in modern systems and how to critically read and implement research ideas.

Learning Objectives

  • Build a clear understanding of speech signal processing foundations.
  • Master core methods in ASR, speaker modeling, synthesis, voice conversion, and separation.
  • Apply machine learning and deep learning methods to speech tasks.
  • Develop and evaluate practical speech technology pipelines.
  • Read, analyze, and present recent research papers in the field.
  • Understand audio and music processing, responsible AI, and large-model-based speech systems.

Prerequisites

  • Basic linear algebra and calculus.
  • Programming experience in Python.
  • Introductory machine learning knowledge.
  • Basic deep learning familiarity (PyTorch or TensorFlow).

Course Materials

Recommended Textbooks

  • Jurafsky D, Martin J H. Speech and Language Processing, 2025 edition.
  • Aalto Speech Processing Book.
  • Huang X, Acero A, Hon H-W. Spoken Language Processing, Prentice Hall, 2011.
  • 韩纪庆、张磊、郑铁然，《语音信号处理》，清华大学出版社。
  • 洪青阳、李琳，《语音识别：原理与应用》，电子工业出版社。

Recommended Reading

Recent papers from top conferences and journals: ICASSP, Interspeech, ACL, T-ASLP, and related venues.

Software & Tooling

  • Python 3.8+ for assignments and projects.
  • PyTorch / TensorFlow for deep learning experiments.
  • Librosa for audio and feature-level processing.
  • WeNet / WeSpeaker / WeSep and related open-source speech toolkits.
  • ESPNet / SpeechBrain / Kaldi for broader ecosystem exposure.
